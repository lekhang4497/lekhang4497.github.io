About

Research

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Information Science at JAIST and a JSPS Research Fellow, working in the Nguyen Laboratory with Prof. Le-Minh Nguyen. My research centers on large language models and natural language processing.

My work aims to bridge the gap between cutting-edge LLMs and their practical deployment in multilingual, resource-constrained environments — especially for non-English languages. This spans efficient inference (speculative decoding, model pruning), question answering, and language resources for low-resource languages such as Vietnamese.

I have published 28 papers (16 as first author), including first-author work at ACL, ECAI, COLING, and LREC, and in Information Processing & Management. My UniSpec paper received the SAC Highlight award at ACL 2026. I was Champion of the Zalo AI Challenge 2023 (runner-up in 2022) and a recipient of the NEC C&C Foundation Grant (2024) and the JSPS Research Fellowship (2026).

Nguyen-Khang Le, Dinh-Truong Do, Le-Minh Nguyen

SPECTRA: Faster Large Language Model Inference with Optimized Internal and External Speculation

Nguyen-Khang Le, Dinh-Truong Do, Le-Minh Nguyen

A training-free framework that combines internal and external speculation to accelerate LLM inference by up to 4.08×, with no modification to the base model.

Paper Press

ANSPRE: Improving Question-Answering in Large Language Models with Answer-Prefix Generation

Nguyen-Khang Le, Dieu-Hien Nguyen, Le-Minh Nguyen

Answer-prefix generation steers LLMs to output concise, exact answer phrases with well-calibrated confidence — important for high-stakes QA in law, medicine, and education.

Paper Press

OptiPrune: Effective Pruning Approach for Every Target Sparsity

Nguyen-Khang Le, Ryotaro Sato, Dai Nakashima, Takeshi Suzuki, Le-Minh Nguyen

A pruning method that stays effective across every target sparsity by adapting non-uniform sparsity and selecting the optimal strategy per regime — validated across datasets, architectures, and languages.

Nguyen-Khang Le, Hien Nguyen, Tung Le Thanh, Le-Minh Nguyen
